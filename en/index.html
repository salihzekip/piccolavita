Production Process

You Place the Order,
We Handle the Rest.

Buying a small house is a big matter of trust. Not knowing what happens after you pay creates anxiety. We do it differently.

1
Contract
We sign a mutual contract. The model, technical specifications, delivery date and payment plan are all put in writing.
2
WhatsApp Group
We open a private WhatsApp group with you. Architects, the production team and you — all in one group, with real-time updates.
3
Architectural Drawing
Our architects prepare a custom drawing for you. We never start production without your approval.
4
Production
Production begins at our Denizli factory. You follow every step through photos and videos we share.
5
Delivery & Installation
Your house is delivered to your land, leveled and set up. The keys are handed to you. 45-60 days, with the first 300 km of transport on us.

Quality & Materials

Materials
We Use.

Every tiny house meets the same technical standard: a sturdy chassis, full insulation, first-class interior craftsmanship, and complete mechanical/electrical fittings. Here's what goes into your house:

Chassis & Load-Bearing Structure
  • 1800 kg × 2 = 3600 kg tandem torsion-bar axle set
  • Coupling, brakes, wheels and jack set (4 units)
  • High-performance steel chassis
  • 7-pin trailer socket and LED electrical signalling components
Insulation
  • 100 mm compressed stone wool on every wall and the roof
  • Dupont Tyvek Solid membrane for heat and moisture protection
  • Plywood floor insulation
  • Sandwich panel roofing with a waterproof membrane
  • PVC frames with anti-fog, solar low-E comfort glass
  • GRP (fibreglass) application inside the shower cabin
  • Aluminium composite panel and thermowood on the exterior
Interior
  • Painted MDF panelling wall covering (color of your choice)
  • Wear-resistant laminate flooring
  • MDF-laminate furniture carcasses with lacquered cabinet doors
  • Scratch-resistant varnished solid-wood kitchen countertop
Mechanical Fittings
  • First-class shower cabin and shower fittings
  • Residential-grade cistern WC (first class)
  • Bathroom vanity with cabinet
  • Residential-grade mechanical fittings (first class)
  • Clean-water and waste-water connection points
  • Granite sink and mixer tap
  • Electrical infrastructure for air conditioning
  • Electric water-heater infrastructure for hot water
Electrical
  • Halogen-free 220V electrical wiring
  • First-class residential-grade electrical fittings
  • LED indoor and outdoor lighting fixtures
  • Outdoor wall lights and fittings
  • Sensor lighting for stairs

Ask a Question

Piccola Vita tiny houses are built on a chassis with O2 type-approved trailer status. Because they're classed as a vehicle, not a building, no building permit is required; they're registered with a towing certificate. Municipal practice can vary depending on where your land is — we brief you in detail before the contract. Details by land type: Is a tiny house legal?

On your own land, rented land, a hobby garden, or a camping/caravan site. Being on wheels, it doesn't require a fixed-structure permit. All you need for installation is level ground and a road suitable for vehicle access.

Yes. Full insulation on the walls, floor and roof, along with insulated double glazing and a vapor barrier, are all standard. Our customers live comfortably in sub-zero temperatures with just air conditioning. On request, we also add a stove or fireplace flue.

Houses are delivered grid-ready: an external electrical connection, clean-water inlet and waste-water outlet are all standard. On land without utilities, we provide project support for solar panels, water tanks and septic-tank solutions.

50% on contract, 50% on delivery. We don't ask for interim payments; you follow the entire process transparently through your own WhatsApp group during production.

An average of 45-60 days after the contract and architectural drawing are approved. The first 300 km of transport is on us; we deliver to all 81 provinces of Turkey.

A comprehensive 24-month warranty from delivery, covering the load-bearing chassis, waterproofing, and the electrical and water systems. We also offer after-sales technical support and periodic maintenance.

All 9 models are fully customizable: exterior cladding, interior materials, furniture, technical fittings and the room layout are all shaped by your choices. Our architects never start production without your approval.
