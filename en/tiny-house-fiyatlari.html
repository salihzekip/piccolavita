Tiny House Prices 2026: Current List and What Changes the Price

Our model-by-model starting prices, real sale prices from delivered projects, and everything that pushes the quote up or down.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · 7 min read · Piccola Vita Tiny House, Denizli

Short answer: As of September 2026, Piccola Vita's towable tiny house prices start at 1,400,000 TL (8 m, 20 sqm) and go up to 1,900,000 TL (10 m, 40 sqm). For custom-delivered houses that range is 1,200,000-2,250,000 TL, and tiny offices start at 950,000 TL. Standard equipment, a 24-month warranty and the first 300 km of transport are all included.

It's hard to give a single number when someone asks about tiny house prices — whether the same 9-meter house has a loft or a ground-floor bedroom, its exterior cladding, or the energy package you choose can shift the price by a few hundred thousand lira. That's why the tables below come in two parts: first, the starting price of our catalog models, then the actual final price of houses we've delivered.

What's included in the price?

What we call the "starting price" isn't an empty shell — it's a livable, move-in-ready house. The standard package includes:

  • Chassis: 1800 kg × 2 tandem torsion-bar axle, brakes, coupling, wheels and jack set, LED signalling
  • Insulation: 100 mm compressed stone wool on every wall and the roof, Dupont Tyvek Solid membrane, PVC frames with low-E thermal glass
  • Exterior: aluminium composite panel and thermowood
  • Interior: painted MDF panelling, laminate flooring, lacquered cabinet doors, solid-wood kitchen countertop, granite sink
  • Bathroom: shower cabin, residential-grade cistern WC, vanity with cabinet
  • Electrical: halogen-free 220V wiring, LED indoor/outdoor lighting, air-conditioning and water-heater infrastructure
  • 24-month warranty and the first 300 km of transport

6 things that change the price

  1. Length and floor area. Every meter between 8, 9 and 10 meters shows up in the price, both in materials and labor. The difference between the Pico 005A (8 m) and 005C (10 m) is 100,000 TL.
  2. Loft or ground-floor bedroom? A loft adds a second sleeping area within the 4-meter height limit, but it means stairs, a railing and extra lighting.
  3. Option packages. Solar power (3.3 kW, 6 × 190 W panels), the off-grid package, inverter AC, appliances, decor and veranda packages are all priced separately. See details on the options page.
  4. Material choices. Exterior cladding, interior colors and furniture changes.
  5. Transport distance. The first 300 km is on us. For provinces farther from Denizli, the remaining distance is quoted separately.
  6. Custom design. If you want a plan outside the catalog, the architectural drawing is prepared from scratch for your needs.

Why are those 300,000-500,000 TL listings online so much cheaper?

We hear this question in almost every conversation. The low prices you see in listings are usually for bare shells (no interior fittings), second-hand units, or chassis without a type-approval certificate. Before comparing prices, I'd ask these four things:

  • Does the chassis have an O2 type-approval certificate? Without one, the house may not count as a "vehicle" either on the road or on your land. We explain why this matters in our legal status guide.
  • How many millimeters of wall and roof insulation, and what material? This determines your winter heating bill.
  • Is the membrane breathable? Most moisture complaints trace back to this (see our damp and moisture article, in Turkish).
  • Is the warranty period and scope written down?

How is payment made?

50% on contract, 50% on delivery. We don't ask for interim payments. Throughout production we share photos and videos of your house through your own WhatsApp group; you make the second payment after seeing the house.

What to budget for besides the house

The tiny house price isn't the only line item. Your land needs level ground and a road a truck can access. If there's no grid connection, you'll need to think about solar for electricity, a tank for water, and a septic solution for waste water. We discuss these together, based on where your land is, before the contract — so nothing comes as a surprise later.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the cheapest tiny house in 2026?

Our most affordable towable tiny house models are the Pico 005A and Pico 004A, starting at 1,400,000 TL. Smaller models used as tiny offices start at 950,000 TL.

Is transport included in tiny house prices?

The first 300 km of transport is included in the price. Beyond 300 km from Denizli, the remaining distance is quoted separately. We deliver to all 81 provinces of Turkey.

Why is the tiny house price given as a "starting price"?

Because the same model varies by bedroom layout, exterior cladding, interior materials and option packages. The starting price is for a livable house delivered with standard equipment.

How does the payment plan work?

50% is paid on contract, 50% on delivery. There are no interim payments; you follow the process with photos and videos throughout production.

How many days does delivery take?

An average of 45-60 days after the contract and architectural drawing are approved.

What does the warranty cover?

24 months from delivery: the load-bearing chassis, waterproofing, and the electrical and water systems.
